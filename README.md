## 5️⃣ AI-Driven Brand Storytelling Campaign – ATZA Digitally

> *Created as part of the AI Content Creator Internship assessment for ATZA Digitally.*

A 55-second cinematic, character-driven AI video based on a Hindi narrative about a struggling painter who discovers new ways to monetize his existing artistic skills after watching the Master App show **“Lakshmi Ka Silai Business.”**

The video combines AI-generated character performance, Hindi voiceover, lip-sync, cinematic B-roll, visual storytelling, and a short-form advertising structure optimized for Instagram Reels.

### 🔗 Watch on YouTube

[▶ Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/shorts/woZALRUAxkA?feature=share)

### ⚙️ Technical Specifications

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Duration | 55 seconds |
| Resolution | 720 × 1280 |
| Aspect Ratio | 9:16 (Instagram Reel) |
| Frame Rate | 24 FPS |
| Language | Hindi |
| Format | Vertical short-form video |
| Style | Cinematic, realistic, character-driven |

### 🛠️ Tools & Workflow

**AI Video Generation**
- `HeyGen` — AI character generation, voice, lip-sync, and video generation

**Creative Direction**
- Structured prompting for character consistency, environments, cinematography, dialogue, and B-roll
- Scene-by-scene visual planning based on the supplied narrative
- Adapted the reference video's style to fit the painting-focused storyline

**Content Strategy**
- Human-interest storytelling
- Financial struggle → realization → transformation → opportunity
- Character-led narrative with B-roll-driven pacing
- Short-form advertising and CTA integration

### 🎥 Scene Breakdown

| Scene | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 1. Financial Struggle | Painter introduces his financial difficulties and daughter's school-fee problem |
| 2. Painter's Life | B-roll of painting, brushes, canvases, and his uncertain income |
| 3. Customer Interaction | Customer asks whether he can create a customized portrait |
| 4. Realization | Painter reflects on how his existing skill could create new opportunities |
| 5. Master App Discovery | Painter discovers the “Lakshmi Ka Silai Business” show |
| 6. Transformation | Customized portraits, new orders, and painting instruction |
| 7. Resolution | Improved financial stability and daughter's fees being paid on time |
| 8. CTA | Painter returns to camera and introduces the Master App opportunity |

### 🎨 Creative Approach

Focused on **realism + emotional storytelling + skill transformation**, using a relatable Indian painter as the central character.

Rather than treating the Master App as the entire subject of the video, the story first establishes a personal problem and then introduces the app as the catalyst for discovering new possibilities.

The visual direction uses a modest Indian painting studio, natural character performances, close-up detail shots, and quick B-roll cuts to create a documentary-style advertising feel.

### 🎬 Production Highlights

- Consistent AI-generated main character
- Hindi character dialogue with lip-sync
- Direct-to-camera storytelling
- Cinematic painting-process B-roll
- Customer interaction and character-driven scenes
- Smartphone/app discovery sequence
- Transformation montage
- Final direct-to-camera CTA
